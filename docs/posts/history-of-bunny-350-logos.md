# Bunny 350 Logo History

!!! note "Moved from William Pang Central"
    This post was originated from my first "blog" site (via Wix cuz I want WYSIWYG) that decided to go abandoned just after this. The exact date was forgotten until I find it archive yay I knew how to archive pages at bare minimum!

This is the history of the Bunny 350 logos.
 
Before the name is changing or creating the first logo, William Pang was drawn the players that named "Bunny350" and "Jumping250".<!-- more -->
 
At 2014, The name "Bunny 350" was created by William Pang, But the original name was "Bunny350".
 
 
The first logo revealed as this.

<img src="/blogs/media/history-of-bunny-350-logos/bunny-350-logo-mid-2014.tiff" alt="Bunny 350 logo introduced on mid 2014"></img>

 The first logo's font was Team Fortress Secondary font.
At Late 2014?, The logo's font was changed from this font to DIN Alternate.
<img src="/blogs/media/history-of-bunny-350-logos/bunny-350-logo-late-2014.tiff" alt="Bunny 350 logo introduced on late 2014"></img>
 Due the first logo is too ugly, the font was changed.
 
At late (September) 2015, The logo was changed again, As seen on Bunny 350's Taken "2015 HK Canon Wonder land" images.
<img src="/blogs/media/history-of-bunny-350-logos/bunny-350-logo-mid-2015.tiff" alt="Bunny 350 logo introduced on mid 2015"></img>
 At 2016, The YouTube name was also renamed from "Bunny350" to "Bunny 350".
Of course, the latest logo has a "strip" design.
