# Summer Smash 2026

Hardware, fun and mod, smashing this summer!

<!-- more -->

From **August 2 to August 30**, Oitswilliam Pang will smash on summer with new hardware, some fun and modding reveal. This includes the upcoming controller that slashes the cabilings, revisiting of left-off mod and a new mod that may shock the high-speed printing industry, again... isn't that journey just done yet?

## New hardware that slashes cablings
The unit in question is called Exhaust Lite Integrated Controller. Its intent is to be powered with just one cable, and not just that as the goal, but just the USB cable. More details will be revealed on August 2, Integration of parts for non-followers will be on October and will release officially to everyone on Christmas of this year.

## Revisiting of left-off mod
On August 16, Oitswilliam will revisit a left-off mod that adds HomeKit and "better" firmware to the air purifier that "broke", by that I mean the fan of the air purifier has stopped working properly for eternity. This time, by revisiting, Oitswilliam attaches the LCD display as the LED matrix wasn't cool, beside glitches.

## The most insane mod that turns UltiMaker printer into a CoreXY monster-of-a-machine
On August 23, Oitswilliam will reveal the plan of the **extremely modded** UltiMaker printer mod that basically with the goal of speed. Because UltiMaker did not make a CoreXY machine, might as well let Oitswilliam do it.

Oitswilliam will also release most FDM / FFF printable parts commonly found in GitHub to MakerWorld and Printables. Oh yea, and also Oitswilliam may upload unexpected content.
