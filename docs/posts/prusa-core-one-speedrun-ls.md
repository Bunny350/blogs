## Log
Stay tuned by [following Oitswilliam Pang in any applicable platforms, Facebook, Instagram and Youtube](https://www.oitswilliam.com/media), where we will have the ~~super-cut version~~ content featuring this machine!

Here's the logs of my build. I never used M3x4r (non-Torx version) of them, so it might be why there is a missing screw hole. The M3x4r is mounted on the bed expansion joint but I never seen it. Might fix it as soon as I can.

I made the panel upside-down, trying to think that the beams are the fault. That was later been fixed. The same apply to the Y axis pulley.

[HD Desiccant is now available in Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1743499-hd-desiccant-holder).
