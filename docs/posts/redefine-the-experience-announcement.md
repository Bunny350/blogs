# Redefine the experience

The date for explaining all of the upcoming hardware has revealed.

<!-- more -->

<img src="/blogs/media/redefine-the-experience-announcement/hero-image.jpg" alt="Image containing EXS, a rectangle with rounded corners and neumorphic experience icon." />

On **June 15**, Oitswilliam Pang will explain more about NUC skirt for OITSWILLIAMV2, the anniversary of the project, and reveal more about the glimpse of neumorphic makeover of one of popular lightweight user environment for Klipper. *Oitswilliam Pang will [discontinue deprecated parts for OITSWILLIAMV2](https://www.oitswilliam.com/blogs/2026/01/30/support-for-reprapdiscount-12864-24v-passive-poe-and-morefine-m6-mount-on-oitswilliamv2-will-be-removed-on-june-15/).*

Oitswilliam Pang will also release EXS for Voron 0 to everyone including those whose never heard of Oitswilliam before, under [OITSWILLIAMV0 project](https://github.com/Bunny350/OITSWILLIAMV0).

*There is a [bonus live-stream](https://www.facebook.com/events/860101013259947/) just two days afterwards. It is not a proper birthday livestream if it's too easy. It is based of the popular start to finish speed-runs for the games. Don't miss it as that will make a RepRap history.*

Stay tuned by [following Oitswilliam Pang in any platforms.](https://www.oitswilliam.com/media).
